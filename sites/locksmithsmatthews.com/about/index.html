Who we are

Matthews Locksmith LLC is a mobile locksmith company based in Matthews, NC. We opened in 2010 and have been helping drivers, homeowners and business owners in Matthews and the surrounding towns ever since. The business is veteran-owned.

We do not run a storefront. Instead, our vans carry the locks, keys, remotes and machines needed to finish the job where you are, whether that is a driveway, an office or a parking lot.

What you can expect

  • A person answers the phone 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
  • A free estimate before any work starts, with a starting price for every service
  • Technicians who explain the options, for example rekeying instead of replacing
  • Service for cars, homes and commercial properties
  • Payment by Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover or cash
