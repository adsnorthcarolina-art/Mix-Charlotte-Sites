Frequently Asked Questions

Are you really open 24 hours?

Yes. Matthews Locksmith answers calls 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays.

Do you come to me?

Yes. We are a mobile locksmith. A technician drives to your home, business or vehicle in Matthews and the surrounding towns with the tools and parts on the van.

How much does a locksmith cost?

Every service has a starting price, listed on its page, for example car lockouts from $65 and lock rekeying from $29. The final price depends on the lock or vehicle, and we confirm it with you before any work begins.

Can you make a car key without the original?

In most cases, yes. We can cut a key to the lock or by code and program it to the vehicle on site, including many push-to-start models.

Should I rekey or replace my locks?

If the locks are in good shape and you only want old keys to stop working, rekeying is the cheaper choice. If the hardware is worn, damaged or low grade, replacing it is better. We can advise when we see the door.

Which areas do you serve?

Matthews, Mint Hill, Stallings, Indian Trail, Weddington, Waxhaw, Monroe, Pineville, the nearby parts of Charlotte, and other towns listed on our service areas page.

How do I pay?
