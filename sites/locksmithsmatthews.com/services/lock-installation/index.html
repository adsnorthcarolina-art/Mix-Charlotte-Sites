Lock & Deadbolt Installation from $55

A deadbolt only protects the door if the bolt throws fully into a solid strike. We drill and fit new locks on wood and metal doors, reinforce the strike, and make sure the latch and bolt line up without forcing the door.

What is included

  • Fresh installation on doors with no existing bore
  • Single and double cylinder deadbolts
  • Strike plate reinforcement
  • Keyed-alike sets for the whole house

Starting price: $55. The final price depends on the lock or vehicle and is confirmed with you before any work begins. The estimate is free.
