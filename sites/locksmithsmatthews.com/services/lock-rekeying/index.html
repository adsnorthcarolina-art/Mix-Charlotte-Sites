Lock Rekeying from $29

Rekeying changes the pins inside the lock so it works with a new key and the old ones no longer open the door. It costs less than replacing the hardware and is the first thing to do after buying a home, losing a key or changing tenants.

What is included

  • Rekeying of deadbolts, knobs and levers
  • All doors keyed to one key if you like
  • New keys cut on the spot
  • Suitable for homes, rentals and businesses

Starting price: $29. The final price depends on the lock or vehicle and is confirmed with you before any work begins. The estimate is free.
